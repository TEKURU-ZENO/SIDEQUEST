# Partner target notes

These are prospects to validate, not confirmed buyers. The first sale should be a limited integration pilot or license discussion. A direct acquisition pitch is premature until SIDEQUEST has production auth, live-stream validation, creator retention data and a repeatable onboarding flow.

| Target | Why it fits | Pitch angle | Best first ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| **StreamElements** | Its creator platform includes overlays, alerts, chatbot, tipping and loyalty. | Add persistent guild progression and creator-approved story episodes to its existing interaction surface. | A technical discovery call and one-creator pilot using its browser-source workflow. |
| **Streamlabs** | It offers widgets, chat, loyalty points and gameful stream widgets. | Offer a new cross-stream game module that turns audience actions into persistent world history. | Evaluate a co-branded widget or API pilot; distinguish SIDEQUEST from alerts, polls and single-session boss widgets. |
| **Twitch Extensions** | Twitch supports panel, overlay and component extension surfaces. | Package a viewer-facing world panel and live participation view for Twitch-native discovery. | Validate extension eligibility, security review and a hosted test before treating it as a channel. |

## Positioning by target

### StreamElements

Lead with continuity: loyalty points already motivate repeat community interaction; SIDEQUEST gives those viewers a shared story and member history that survives between broadcasts. Do not pitch SIDEQUEST as a replacement for its loyalty wallet or chatbot.

### Streamlabs

Lead with a distinct game format: persistent chapters and deterministic group encounters. Streamlabs already offers alerts, polls, chat and gameful widgets, so a generic “more engagement” claim is too weak. Show the long-lived world state and creator controls.

### Twitch

Lead with viewer participation in an extension, not an acquisition thesis. Twitch Extensions are sandboxed webpages available in panel, overlay and component placements. Security requirements and extension review need early validation.

## Claims to avoid until a pilot proves them

- Increased retention, watch time, revenue or viewer return rate.
- Production-ready multi-tenant security or a generally available extension.
- Live Twitch reward reliability or cross-platform support.
- Guaranteed user growth or a proven licensing price.
