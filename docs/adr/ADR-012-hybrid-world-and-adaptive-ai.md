# ADR-012: Hybrid persistent world and adaptive AI

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** Persistent history creates continuity; adaptive AI can turn that history into relevant chapters without becoming the rules authority.

**Decision:** Combine a deterministic, persistent world and encounter engine with bounded AI direction over historical read models.

**Alternatives:** Pure scripted campaign (reliable but less responsive); AI-generated game with model-owned state (adaptive but hard to trust and replay).

**Consequences:** Save factual events and decisions as authoritative history. Use AI for proposals, summaries and narration, validated and overridable by the streamer.
